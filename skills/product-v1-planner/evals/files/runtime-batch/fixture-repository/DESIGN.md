# Research Brief v1

## Requirements

- `REQ-CAPTURE-1`: Capture a source URL and capture timestamp.
- `REQ-REVIEW-1`: A reviewer can correct a claim while retaining its source and correction history.
- `REQ-EXPORT-1`: Export a reviewed brief locally with provenance intact.

## Completion

v1 is complete when a captured source moves through review and local export
without losing provenance or correction history.
